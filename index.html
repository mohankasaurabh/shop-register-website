Local work. Optional cloud copies.

Shop records are stored locally. The Google connection sends backups to your Google Drive. Computers keep independent records; backups are not live synchronization.

Why Shop Register asks for Google access

Google sign-in identifies your backup account. File access lets the application create, find, restore and clean up its own backup files. It does not request access to your entire Drive or your Gmail.
